Stop Failing

One day at a time. Private on your device.

Your data stays in this browser and is never uploaded. If urges feel overwhelming or hard to control, talking to a counsellor or doctor can help.

Ride out the urge

Urges peak and pass, usually within 15 to 20 minutes.

Get ready
  • Stand up and leave the room you are in.
  • Put your phone down or switch to another app.
  • Drink water, then walk or do 20 push-ups.

Log a slip?

Your current streak is saved to your history and a new one starts. A slip is data, not failure.
